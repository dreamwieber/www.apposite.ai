Soft Break Privacy Policy

Soft Break doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the game never sends anything about you to us or anyone else.

Your best score and your sound and haptics settings are saved on your device so they're there next time. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are posted to Soft Break's leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. You can turn Game Center off in Settings at any time.

For questions, contact: hello@apposite.ai
