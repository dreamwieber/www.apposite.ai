Note names won’t tune your ukulele.

Knowing the strings should read G C E A is the easy half. Landing each frequency — and knowing whether you are nine cents flat or three — is the half that needs an ear or an instrument.

3

Changing tension on one string shifts the neck slightly. The rest usually need a nudge.

What Low G is for

Adds a fifth of range below the C string, which fingerstyle and jazz players use for real basslines. It usually needs a dedicated low-G string — often wound — since a standard high-G string cannot reach the pitch.
